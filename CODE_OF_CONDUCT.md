# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Contributors are expected to communicate professionally, stay focused on the work, and help maintain a practical engineering culture.

Examples of good behavior:

- Giving specific, actionable feedback
- Respecting different experience levels
- Keeping technical debate evidence-based
- Correcting mistakes without personal attacks
- Being honest about implementation status and limitations

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal insults, and knowingly misleading users or contributors.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may result in being blocked from participation.
